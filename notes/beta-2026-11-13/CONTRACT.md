## 2. Shared contracts (everyone codes to these, nobody waits)
2.1 `CT.setDoc(col,id,obj)` returns a Promise resolving to `true` on success, `false` on failure (never rejects). It updates `CT.data[col]` locally at once (optimistic) and rolls back on failure. Callers that show success ("Stratum stained.", "Saved") must await it and show an error toast instead when it resolves `false`. `CT.toast(msg, kind)` with kind `'err'` for errors (D02 adds kind support; D01 styles `.toast.err`).
2.2 Option shuffle: stain tests, re-stains, duels (D05) and Compare (D04) show options in a shuffled order that is stable for that question within the session view (store the order in CT.ui), and score against the original index. Book devs ALSO rebalance answer keys in source so `a` is spread evenly over 0–3 (move the right option, update `a`; keep explanations consistent with letters if they mention any).
2.3 Questions may carry optional fields: `d` (subject: 'path'|'micro'|'pharm'|'bio'|'phys') and `c` (a concept id that exists in BANK). Book devs (D07–D16) add `d` to EVERY question and `c` where a matching concept exists (list ids with: `node -e 'global.window=global;["bank1","bank2","bank3","bank4","bank5","bank6a","bank6b","bank6c","bank7","bank8","bank9"].forEach(f=>{try{require("/home/claude/citron/"+f+".js")}catch(e){}});console.log(BANK.C.map(c=>c.id).join(" "))'`). D05's logMiss uses `q.c || chapter.c[0]` and `q.d`.
2.4 Seal rule (D05): once a depth's boss is won, its seal stays broken even if new strata are added to that depth later. New strata in a depth whose seal is already broken are optional extra reading (shown, stainable, not blocking). For depths not yet sealed, all strata count.
2.5 Boss lines and ending: D06 adds to each `DEPTHS[i].boss` optional `intro`, `win`, `lose`: arrays of `{from:'hale'|'probe'|'static'|'pall'|'host', t:'text'}`. D05 shows boss intro on the duel start screen, win/lose lines in the result panel (pick one at random if several), and after the final boss is won offers "Hear the ending" → route `#end` which renders STORY.END as a sequence; D03 adds `end` to the route regex in app-today.js parseHash (`/^(depth-\d+|ch-.+|restain-.+|duel-\d+|host|radio|end)$/`) and D05 implements the view (CT.views or however dive routes are rendered today — read the code).

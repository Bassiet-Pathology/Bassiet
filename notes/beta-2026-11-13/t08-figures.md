# t08 — Interactive figures (figs.js … figs6.js, 26 figures)

Tester t08. All 26 figures were rendered at 375 px (phone) and 1440 px (desktop) in several control states each: 128 states in all, in the dive world, plus 5 figures in Lights-on/light. I also checked keyboard focus in a real chapter (#ch-c1-membrane) and read every model function against Step 1 physiology.
Scripts: `beta/work-t08/` (sheets.js, sheets-light.js, focus.js, coag.js, serve.js). Contact sheets: `beta/shots/t08-<fig>-375.png`, `t08-<fig>-1440.png`, `t08-<fig>-p0.png` (phone, split into columns), `t08-light-sheet.png`.
Note: the local file has no `<meta charset>`, so over file:// every ⁺ and ₂ shows as mojibake ("Naâº"). I served the files as UTF-8 to judge the real look. This is a local-only issue, but adding `<meta charset="utf-8">` to index.html costs nothing.

## 1. Verdict — score 5/10

The figure set has good breadth, and several figures really teach: the PK accumulation curve, the dose–response curves, the visual fields, the cord lesions and the menstrual cycle. The models are mostly correct. Four problems hold it back. **(1)** A string-matching bug makes the coagulation cascade show **factor V as vitamin-K dependent, and factors VII and V as affected in haemophilia A**, which is wrong on a core Step 1 fact. **(2)** On a phone, every label inside an SVG renders at **6.4–8.9 px**, which is unreadable. Two tables inside figures hide their most important columns off-screen. **(3)** Three figures contradict their own chapters: the MI timeline is off by one stage, the ischaemia morphology is scaled to the wrong clock, and the ABG reader skips the respiratory compensation rules that the chapter teaches. **(4)** About a third of the figures are captions that swap text when you press a button (V/Q, nephron, ans, bili, repair). Moving a control does not reveal the mechanism. Touch targets are 34 px buttons and 16 px sliders, and keyboard focus is lost on every button press.

## 2. What works well
- One shared state model. `CT.ui.fig` keeps each figure's state when you switch stains or re-render, and sliders keep focus because only `.fv` is rebuilt on input.
- **pk** (accumulation, loading dose, the 4–5 half-life rule, fluctuation) and **drc** (right shift vs lowered maximum) are accurate, and moving a control shows the mechanism.
- **visual**: all six field defects are correct (bitemporal, homonymous, pie-in-the-sky, parietal inferior, macular sparing), drawn as the patient sees them.
- **cord**: the tract sets for Brown-Séquard, syrinx, subacute combined degeneration, tabes, ASA and ALS are all correct.
- **cycle**: the curve shapes are right. Oestradiol peaks before the LH surge, there is a small mid-cycle FSH peak, and progesterone dominates the luteal phase.
- **bili**, **nephron**, **ans** and **anemia**: the content is accurate and Step 1-relevant (Gilbert, Crigler-Najjar, Dubin-Johnson, Bartter/Gitelman/Liddle, Mentzer-style sorting).
- **etc**: the meters (flow, O₂ use, gradient, ATP, heat) contrast oligomycin and uncouplers well.
- No console errors in any state, no page-level horizontal overflow, and the desktop rendering is clean (the SVG is capped at 260 px tall, so labels read at about 15 px).

## 4. Chapters without a figure that would gain most (top 8, with designs)

76 chapters, 25 with a figure (c1-unmaking has two). Ranked by Step 1 yield × how much an interactive helps:

1. **c9-exchange "The Thin Walls" — Oxygen–haemoglobin dissociation** (`F.o2hb`). Hill curve `SO₂=PO₂ⁿ/(PO₂ⁿ+P50ⁿ)`, n=2.7, P50 26.7. Toggles: ↑CO₂/↓pH, ↑temp, ↑2,3-BPG (each shifts P50 +3–4), exercise (all three), HbF (P50 19), CO 30% (left shift + content ceiling 70%), methaemoglobin, anaemia (Hb 7). A second y-axis shows O₂ content `1.34×Hb×SO₂+0.003×PO₂`. Markers at PO₂ 100 (artery) and 40 (vein) show the O₂ unloaded as a shaded bar; the P50 is read out. ## 5. Top 5 improvement ideas for the figures

1. **A phone layout for every figure.** Give each `view(s,W)` a width argument (`CT.figW()` = 340 below 560 px, 460 otherwise), raise the font floor to 11 rendered px, stack chain-type figures (apoptosis, bili, coag, etc.) vertically on narrow screens, and turn in-figure tables into label:value grids. Add a headless test that fails if any `<text>` renders under 11 px or overlaps another, or if an SVG text box exceeds its viewBox. The script in `work-t08/sheets.js` already computes these metrics and can be reused.
2. **"Before vs after" by default.** In every figure with a baseline (cardap, drc, pvloop, neuroap, pk, vq, spiro), draw the baseline as a faint ghost and the changed state in accent, with a caption that names the difference ("ESV +35 mL, SV −35 mL"). This turns button-pressing into seeing the mechanism.
3. **A mechanism mode for the lookup figures.** repair, nephron, bili, ans and leuko currently swap captions. Give each a 3–4 step "Play" control that animates the process: excise → fill → seal; ion transport across a tubule cell, with the drug block shown; haem → bilirubin → conjugation → bile → urobilinogen → urine/stool, with the blockage point; receptor occupancy bars for α1/α2/β1/β2/M with the organ effects lighting up; and the molecules in leukocyte adhesion.
4. **Predict-then-reveal inside figures.** Before a state change, ask "What happens to X?" with ↑ / ↓ / no-change chips, then animate the answer. Log misses to the Hull with the figure id, so wrong guesses become re-stain material (this fits the app's Predict practice mode and its spaced re-stains).
5. **One shared figure toolkit and theme.** Move `btn`, `rng`, `svg`, `meter` and `legend` into one shared helper; give every slider a human label, a `for` association and a wide thumb; add `aria-live` captions and state-aware `aria-label`s; replace hard-coded colours with tokens; and keep focus on the pressed control (t08-11). Then audit all 26 figures against one checklist: units on axes, a legend whenever colour carries meaning, nothing told by colour alone, and no figure without a visible cause→effect link.

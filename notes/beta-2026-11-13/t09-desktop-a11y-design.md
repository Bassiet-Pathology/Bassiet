# t09: Visual design, desktop/tablet layout and accessibility

Tester: t09 · Date: 13/11/2026 · Build: /home/claude/citron (index.html 35.8 KB, sw `bassiet-v4`)
Method: Playwright/Chromium. Viewports: 1440×900, 1920×1080, 820×1180, 1180×820 and 390×844. Worlds: The dive, plus Lights on with OS light and OS dark. Text sizes S/N/L, Pall drift on/off, `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. Progress was seeded (depths 1–2 sealed, depth 3 stained with its boss waiting, one fading stain, misses, 9-day streak). I tested three builds:
- **Q**: the local file as shipped (quirks mode).
- **D**: the same file with `<!doctype html>` prepended.
- **P**: "published-like", with doctype, `lang="en"`, `<meta charset="utf-8">` and a viewport meta.

WCAG ratios were computed from every token block in index.html (script: `beta/work-t09/contrast.js`, full table: `beta/work-t09/contrast.md`).
Screenshots: `beta/shots/t09-*.png`. Scripts: `beta/work-t09/`.
Caveat: Google Fonts is blocked in this sandbox, so every screenshot uses the fallbacks (Inter for body/display, DejaVu Sans Mono). I could not judge Pixelify Sans as rendered. Font-loading findings are based on the code.

---

## 1. Verdict

**Score: 6/10 for design, layout and accessibility.**
- The dive world's core palette is right: warm near-black, amber lamp glow, hue-tinted pixel banners, soft-glowing stain plates and a calm 75-character reading column. The top of the Dive home, the depth banners and the chapter reader do feel premium and mysterious.
- The mood breaks once you leave those screens:
  - the "Instruments" block on the home page (a candy-coloured coverage grid, the old Today dashboard);
  - Atlas, Practice, Plates and Hull, which are still the generic app;
  - a plain duel screen.
- One layout bug ruins the default phone experience: in the dive world the bottom tab bar is drawn at the top, over the brand (t09-01).
- Accessibility has solid basics (real buttons, `aria-pressed`, `aria-current`, a visible amber focus ring in the dark themes, no horizontal overflow at any size). It is let down by:
  - focus being thrown to `<body>` after every action;
  - right/wrong shown by colour alone in Trace and Predict;
  - an untimeable duel timer;
  - several WCAG contrast failures (white text on the bright subject fills, light-theme danger button and focus ring);
  - a static page title with no focus move when the route changes.

## 2. What works well

- **Tokens:** the token system is clean, and all three themes pass AA for body, muted, accent-text and every stain label/body pair (dive: 7.9–15.2:1).
- **Reader:** `.sec>p` at 17.5px/1.68 gives about 75 characters per line on desktop and tablet, and 39 on phone. The `max-width:66ch` and `.reader{max-width:780px}` measures are good.
- **Stain plates:** each callout carries a text label ("TEAL STAIN · Biochemistry"), so stains are never colour-only. The dive glow (`box-shadow:0 0 22px -12px var(--sc)`) looks lovely.
- **Banners:** the pixel scenes, depth icons and dashed shaft line give a strong identity. The Pall canvas stops in reading/duel mode, when motion is set to Off, and under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Semantics:**
  - nav has `aria-label` and `aria-current`;
  - the lamp is a `role=group` with an `aria-label`;
  - toggle buttons use `aria-pressed`;
  - coverage cells have full `aria-label`s;
  - figures are real `<figure>`/`<figcaption>`;
  - the concept web nodes are focusable (`role=button tabindex=0`) and work with Enter/Space.
- **Layout safety:** no horizontal page scroll at 390–1920 at any text size. Safe-area insets are handled, and phone touch targets mostly meet 44px.
- **Doctype:** adding a doctype changes almost nothing. Page heights differ by at most 55px, all from the coverage-table cell line-height. No layout depends on quirks.

   - One `CT.render` wrapper that restores focus to the equivalent control after re-render (t09-03).

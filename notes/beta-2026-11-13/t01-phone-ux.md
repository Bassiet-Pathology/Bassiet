# t01 — Phone experience as a brand-new user

Tester: t01 · Sizes: 390×844 (main), 375×667 (iPhone SE), 412×915 (large Android) · Date: 13/11/2026
Method: Playwright Chromium with `isMobile`/`hasTouch`, DPR 2. The source `index.html` has no doctype, charset or viewport meta, so opened directly it renders in quirks mode at a 980 px desktop layout with garbled "âˆ’" characters. To match the published artifact, I tested a copy wrapped in `<!doctype html><meta charset="utf-8"><meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width,initial-scale=1,viewport-fit=cover">`. I also served it over local HTTP to test the service worker. The source was not edited. Google Fonts were blocked in the sandbox, so the screenshots show fallback fonts (Pixelify Sans was not judged).
Scripts: `/tmp/claude-0/-home-claude/c80d3a2e-1d1d-5dac-83ee-172c002ca966/scratchpad/beta/work-t01/` (a.js, b.js, c.js, d.js). Screenshots: `.../beta/shots/t01-*.png` (prefixes p-, se-, an-).

## 1. Verdict

The world looks right on a phone. The type is large and calm, the stain callouts glow without hurting readability, the chapter reader is a pleasure to read, and contrast is good almost everywhere (muted text is 7.1:1 on the background). But the default dive world has a real layout bug: the bottom nav bar is drawn at the **top** of the screen, on top of the brand. The opening briefing from Hale also disappears within ~50 ms, so a new user only ever sees line 4 of 4. The radio pop-up covers answer options and hides the confirmation toasts. Onboarding never states the core loop (read → 5-question stain test ≥60% → all strata → duel → seal breaks), and Hale's first line suggests the opposite mechanic. These are all cheap fixes.

**Score: 5/10** (would be 8/10 once the five fixes in the summary are made).

## 2. What works well

- Reader typography: 17.5 px body, 1.68 line height and a ~66ch measure read very well at 390 px. Bold terms (`#FFF6E8`) stand out without shouting.
- Colour contrast: muted text on the background is 7.1:1, the epigraph 11.2:1, the Pall transmission 11.2:1 and accent ink on orange 8.9:1. No real failures except the deliberately dim locked depths.
- No horizontal page scroll at 375, 390 or 412 px, including at Large text.
- Option buttons, strata rows, `.btn` and depth rows are all ≥44 px on phone (the `max-width:759px` min-height rule works).
- Answering a question keeps the scroll position (`keepY`). Toggling a stain keeps scrollY. Changing a look option keeps scrollY. After a pass, the user lands on the depth page at the top, which is correct.
- "Lights on" works fully, and in that world the bottom nav is correctly at the bottom (788 px).
- The duel screen on 390 px fits: question, four 48 px options, then the explanation and Next (top 736 px) are all visible without scrolling.
- Re-stain flow, the fading panel and the duel loss screen ("Nothing is lost. Study the wounds below") are clear and kind.
- The service worker registers and activates, and caches 47 files under `bassiet-v4`. The manifest links correctly.

## 4. Top 5 improvement ideas

1. **First-dive onboarding sequence (≈60 s).** For a user with zero reads, replace Home with a full-screen "carrier found" sequence: the four start lines appear one by one (tap to advance, honouring Motion off). It ends on a single diagram card of the loop, "Read a stratum → stain test ≥60% → all strata stained → duel the avatar → seal breaks → next depth", and one button, "Begin the descent". Normal Home shows from the second visit.
2. **A real phone reader mode.** Keep header and nav only. Turn the lamp into one floating "◐ Lamp" chip that opens a sheet with the five subject toggles. Add a thin reading-progress bar under the header and a "Jump to stain test ↓" chip once 70 % is scrolled. Hide the bottom nav while scrolling down and show it on scroll up. This recovers ~140 px on iPhone SE.

## Format checks (contract 2.7, 2.3, 2.2, 2.10)
- Each chapter has 5–6 sections, each section has a table and a REMEMBER key (c15 "Tics" has no table), and there are 8–10 stain callouts per chapter covering all five stain types across the set.
- Each chapter has 7 questions. Every question has `d`, and every question has a `c` that exists in BANK once bank9.js is loaded (see the request below). Every explanation is 2–3 sentences and says why the answer is right and why the most tempting distractor is wrong.
- Prose word counts (p only): c13 1204, c14 1350, c15 1438, c16 1374. Three chapters run 150–240 words over the 1200 guide. I cut once already from 1850, 1596 and 1467, and I kept the rest so the chapters stay at textbook depth. Most of the facts are also in the tables, keys and stains.
- British spelling, no emoji, original wording, and no commercial mnemonics. The REMEMBER keys are my own phrasings. Nothing touches the story twist: the epigraphs only describe the place.
- `node --check` passes.

That is 22 chapters, 70 sections, every stain callout, table and REMEMBER box, and all 110 stain-test questions (q / o / a / x). Source was read only. Helper scripts are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-claude/c80d3a2e-1d1d-5dac-83ee-172c002ca966/scratchpad/beta/work-t05/`.

## 1. Verdict — score 7 / 10

The medicine is very accurate. I found **no answer key that is wrong**. I found only a handful of real factual slips: the CRH-test wording in Cushing work-up, the lacunar-stroke artery for pure-sensory stroke, "non-bizarre" delusions, and the definition of placenta accreta.

The two main weaknesses are elsewhere:
- **Depth.** The text is a dense high-yield summary (First-Aid density, in original wording), not a textbook. Several large Step 1 blocks are missing altogether: MSK anatomy and paediatric orthopaedics, cranial nerves and brainstem lesion localisation, aphasia types, eating disorders, child psychiatry, reproductive embryology and gametogenesis, and general anaesthetics.
- **The stain tests can be gamed.** Options are never shuffled, and 97 of 110 answers in these depths are option 2.

## 2. What works well

- Facts are up to date in many places most apps get wrong:
  - the "rule of 10s is outdated" note for pheochromocytoma
  - prolonged grief disorder at ≥12 months (DSM-5-TR)
  - tenecteplase as well as alteplase, and thrombectomy up to 24 h
  - lecanemab
  - semaglutide with its cardiovascular benefit
  - SGLT2 euglycaemic DKA
  - HLA-B*58:01 and HLA-B*15:02
  - romosozumab, valbenazine and deutetrabenazine, edaravone, nusinersen
- Discriminating tables are excellent: MG vs Lambert-Eaton, the calcium / phosphate / PTH matrix, the bone-disease lab matrix, the EPS timing table, CAH, the cord-lesion table and the bleed table.
- The stain callouts truly join the subjects together. Examples: path ↔ pharm in thyroid, micro in TORCH, and the CK isoforms in the muscle chapter.
- The questions are clean vignettes with one defensible answer. The explanations match the keys.
- Cross-references to other depths resolve (Scar Fields = c2-scar; obstructive sleep apnoea is in Alveolar Caves c9). SIADH, DKA and SLE, which I first marked as gaps, are covered in c10-salts, c10-acid and c4.
